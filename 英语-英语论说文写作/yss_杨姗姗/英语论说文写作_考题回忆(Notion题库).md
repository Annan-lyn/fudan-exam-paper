# 英语论说文写作（杨姗姗）考题回忆

Discuss one of the following statements. Clearly state your position and flesh it out into an article. (400-600 words; 100 points)
- In terms of social progress, what is the most important thing that humanity still has to overcome in your opinion?
- Should people choose one of two opposing sides of an issue, or is the truth usually found "in the middle"?
